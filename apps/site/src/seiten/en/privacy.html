Privacy Policy

for the website staygrid.cloud and the “StayGrid” app for iOS and Android · Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung. If the two versions differ, the German version prevails.

In short: The app is a work tool for staff of hotels that use StayGrid. On the phone it stores only your sign-in, protected by the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore. Face ID and fingerprint are checked by your phone alone. Photos of business cards are analysed on the device and never uploaded. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Our servers are located in Germany.

2. Two roles: app provider and service provider to the hotel

StayGrid is software for accommodation businesses. The app is intended for their staff. Accounts are created by the hotel; there is no sign-up inside the app.

  • As provider of the app and the website we are the controller for what is needed to provide and securely operate them: sign-in, sessions, abuse prevention and delivering the website (sections 3 to 6).
  • For the content you work with in StayGrid (reservations, guests, registration forms, invoices, cash book, cleaning schedule, your user account and working time), the hotel you work for is the controller. We process this data only on its behalf and according to its instructions (Art. 28 GDPR). Requests about this data are best addressed to the hotel; we support it in answering them.

3. What the app processes

Sign-in and session

To sign in, you send your email address or user name and your password. We store the password only as a cryptographic hash. After signing in, the app receives a random session token. It is kept on your phone in the Keychain (iOS) or encrypted with a key from the Android Keystore, and it is excluded from device backups.

For each session our server stores: the user account, start and last activity, the IP address at sign-in and the device and operating system identifier sent by the app (user agent, at most 300 characters). This lets you and the hotel see signed-in devices and sign them out.

A session ends when you sign out, when the hotel ends it, after 30 days without use, and at the latest after 180 days. It is deleted seven days later.

We count failed sign-in attempts per account and origin to prevent password guessing. These counters are deleted seven days after the last failed attempt.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app you use) and Art. 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in secure operation).

Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint

The app locks when it starts and after five minutes in the background, and it can be unlocked with your phone’s screen lock. The check is performed solely by the operating system. The app only learns whether it succeeded; neither the app nor we receive any biometric data.

Camera: scanning business cards

When you trigger it, the app photographs a business card to suggest a company’s name and address. Text recognition runs on the device: Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android, each with a bundled model. The photo is not sent to us and is not stored by the app. Only what you accept into the form and save reaches the server. You can revoke camera access at any time in your phone’s settings.

Photos you deliberately take or select for upload, such as a receipt in the cash book, are different: that single image is sent to the server and stored on behalf of the hotel. The app only sees the image you pick, not your photo library.

Note on Android: Under Google’s terms, Google ML Kit sends device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, version) and performance and usage metrics of the text recognition to Google in order to operate and improve the service. Images and recognised text are not sent. The recipient is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; a transfer to the USA is possible and covered by the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our interest is text recognition that works without uploading the photo.

Files

When you open an invoice or an export, the app places the file in the app’s cache and hands it to your phone’s preview or share sheet. Only the most recently opened file is kept there; it is replaced the next time. What you pass on via the share sheet is up to you.

Readable copy during network outages

So that work can continue during a network outage, the app keeps a read-only copy of the arrivals list, the in-house list and the room status for yesterday, today and the coming days on the device. It removes older days by itself and deletes the copy entirely when you sign out or the session expires.

Notifications

The app currently does not send push notifications. If this feature is added, we will update this policy beforehand.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics or crash reporting
  • No access to location, contacts, microphone, calendar or your photo library as a whole
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties
  • No payment card data and no copies of ID documents, not even on behalf of the hotel

App Store and Google Play

When you download the app, Apple or Google process data under their own responsibility, such as your account and the download. If you have agreed in your phone’s settings, Apple and Google provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you. See the privacy notices of Apple and Google for details.

5. The website staygrid.cloud

When you visit this site, our server processes your IP address and your browser’s request data, because it cannot deliver the page otherwise (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not keep an access log. The site sets no cookies, loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers and uses no analytics. If you email us, we use your details to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is closed and no retention obligation applies.

6. Retention

  • Sessions: until seven days after they end (see section 3)
  • Failed sign-in counters: seven days after the last failed attempt
  • User account: as long as the hotel keeps it
  • Content processed on behalf of the hotel: according to its instructions and statutory periods, e.g. ten years for invoices under § 147 of the German Fiscal Code
  • Data on the phone: until you sign out or delete the app

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21). Write to datenschutz@staygrid.cloud. If your request concerns data we process on behalf of a hotel, we forward it to the hotel.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.

8. Deleting your account

Your user account belongs to the hotel that created it. To have it deleted, contact the hotel or datenschutz@staygrid.cloud; we will take care of it together with the hotel. To remove the data on your phone, sign out or delete the app.

9. Security

All transmission is encrypted. Data of different hotels is separated in the database by row-level security policies, ID document numbers are stored encrypted, and backups leave the server only in encrypted form.

10. Children

The app is a work tool and is not directed at children.
